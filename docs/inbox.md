# Inbox

Sift's dashboard was built to answer what Sift could not decide. Inbox makes it the place to read and handle mail too, with what Sift knows about each message beside it: its label, how sure it is, the sender's standing, and rules. It is optional. Turned on, Sift keeps a copy of the person's mail, as far back as they choose, and goes to Gmail only to change something or to fetch mail it does not have yet; and it asks Google for leave to send as them.

## What it is for

- Reading and answering mail as fast as in Gmail: a list, a reader, a composer, keys for everything.
- Seeing mail the way Sift sees it: every row says what Sift thinks it is, and the list narrows by that.
- Correcting Sift where you read, not on a separate screen: the reader every list opens has the answer and Rule… on one line under the title.

## The frame

On a desktop the sections move from the masthead to a slim column on the left (`Rail`, `web/src/system/rail.tsx`): the wordmark at its top, with Inbox on Compose under it, then one icon per section with its name under it (For review, Inbox, Activity, Labels, Rules), the current one marked in the highlighter. For review carries how many wait. At its foot: Sift's line (sync and cost, opening the same card as before), the mailbox switcher, and the avatar's menu: the switcher sits with the account whose mailboxes it chooses between, under the line, which stays for all of them. The page title sits at the top of the page, where the masthead was, and a `Notice` over it.

On a phone nothing moves from the bar and menu, which gain Inbox and the switcher, and Inbox's header has Compose (`PhoneCompose`, `web/src/views/inbox.tsx`).

## Mailboxes

A person may have several Gmail accounts connected. Everywhere a list shows mail, it is every mailbox's, unless the switcher at the foot of the rail narrows it to one. The choice is the reader's convenience, kept in the browser, and sent with every request as `mailbox` (`narrow`, `src/dashboard/scope.ts`). A row says which mailbox its mail is in only while the switcher shows all of them and there is more than one.

## The list

Inbox lists conversations, newest first, under the same periods as For review. A row: who (the senders of the conversation, the reader's own name as "me"), how many messages, the subject and the start of the newest text, what Sift thinks it is (a `Meter` and the label), and when. Unread is in bold. Rows select with a check; selected rows get Archive, Mark read and Mark unread, and Label.

Keys, as Gmail's: J and K move, O or Enter opens, X selects, E archives, Shift+I and Shift+U mark read and unread, C writes a new message, / searches, Z undoes; in the reader R replies, A replies to all and F forwards.

Built (`web/src/views/inbox.tsx`, `app/(dashboard)/inbox/page.tsx`): the list, its views (Everything, Needs you, Sorted), the Label and Unread filters, the search, the period headings, and loading the next page as the end of the list is reached (`More`). A row's check is `RowCheck`; selected rows put Archive, Mark read and Mark unread, with E, Shift+I and Shift+U, in the search bar's place (`ListView`'s `selected`), so the rows do not move. The keys act on what is selected: X, then E. On a phone a row reads as For review's and has no check; the reader has the presses. Label is not built. What Sift thinks is the newest message's current decision, or the reader's answer about it where they gave one (an answer is a `feedback` row on the decision, not a decision of its own).

## Sift's controls

What Sift adds to an inbox, in the order it costs:

1. **What it is.** Every message Sift decided has a label and a sureness. The list filters by label and by sureness (Sure, Needs you), and the views at the end of the bar are Everything, Needs you (what Sift would leave in the inbox or could not place) and Sorted (what it would move out).
2. **Ask.** Words typed into the search that are not a search ("invoices from vendors this month") become a filter, shown as the filter it made, which the reader can change. One model call a question; nothing is sent to the model but the words, the reader's today and labels, and the sender lookups the rule drafter makes too.

The model only reads. It never sends, drafts or replies, and is given no tool that does. Ask refuses while Inbox is off or no model is connected, and never runs unless pressed.

Built: Ask (`useAsk`, `web/src/components/ask.tsx`; `POST /api/mail/ask`; `createMailAsker`, `src/model/ask.ts`). Pressing Ask at the end of the search box, or Enter in it, sends the words with the reader's today to the model, which may look senders up and answers through one tool with a filter of what the list takes: senders (addresses or domains) any of which wrote a message of the conversation, a label, a view, unread, a first day, and words one message must have (`readMailFilter`, `src/triage/mail-ask.ts`): "the invoice from March about the roof" is invoice and roof, from the first of March. A sender no look-up returned is sent back once as unusable, so the filter never narrows to an address the model made up. The words become the search (`q`) and the rest goes into the address (`from`, `domain`, `since`, beside `label`, `view` and `unread`), each a chip that clears it, as any filter. What it says it cannot narrow by (what the reader replied to, whom they wrote to) is a note over the list; with nothing to narrow by, that note is all it answers. `GET /api/mail/threads` finds senders by their keyed hashes in SQL (`from`, `domain`, comma-separated) and a first day as the newest message's time (`since`). Every word must be in one message, so the model is told to give only the few that must be there and never alternatives: "invoices or receipts" cannot be said. Each call is a `mail_asks` row: the words and the answer encrypted, look-ups, model, tokens and cost.

## Search

The search finds conversations one of whose messages has every word typed, in its text, its sender's name or address, or its subject, in any order, case and accents aside ("helene" finds Hélène), each a whole word but the last, which may be the start of one ("invo" finds invoice; typed alone, from three letters). It looks through all the mail Sift keeps, not only the inbox, and so do Ask's senders and first day; Spam and Trash it leaves out, as Gmail does. A conversation found outside the inbox says so on its row, quietly before the subject: Archived, or Sent when every message of it is the person's own. Newest first, by the conversation's newest message, as the list.

The text is encrypted, so the search reads an index of it (`mail_bodies.search`, `src/store/search.ts`) made as the text is written: by the copy, sync and the pass (`recordKept`, `keepText`, `src/store/kept.ts`) and for what the person sends (`recordSent`). Each message's index is a Bloom filter: its words, its sender's and its subject's (`wordsOf`, `src/search.ts`), each whole and each start of each from three letters to six, each setting 7 bits chosen by a keyed hash (`searchHash`, `src/secrets.ts`), in a filter of about 12 bits a term rounded up to a power of two, from 128 bytes to 4 KB. A search turns its words into the same terms and asks Postgres for the conversations with a message whose filter has all their bits (`get_bit`, `mayMatch`); that is true of every message that matches and of some that do not, one in a few hundred to one in tens of thousands for each word, so the text of what it lets through is opened, newest first, and read for the words (`matches`), until a page and one match. A start longer than six letters is looked up by its first six, and one shorter than three after other words by those words alone, and each checked in the text. What it reveals: [security.md](security.md#the-search-index).

Being in the same row as the text, the index goes in the same write as the text whenever that goes: Inbox turned off, the window narrowed, retention. Text kept before there was an index has none (`search` null) and is not found until a pass indexes it: 1,000 a pass, begun by the look while any is left (`searchDue`, `indexKept`). A new key makes every index again with the text, in the rekey ([secrets.md](secrets.md)). What it costs: [runtime.md](runtime.md#what-it-costs).

## The reader

One reader for every list (`MessageReader`, `web/src/views/reader.tsx`, from the parts `Reader`, `ReaderBar`, `AnswerRow`, `MessageStack` and `ReplyBox`): opening a message shows it in the list's place, inside the frame, on the list's own route with `?message=`. The list stays on the page, hidden, so Back and Esc return to it as it was, with the focus on the row of the message last shown. Inbox opens a conversation, with `thread` beside `message` (its newest in the inbox) in the address: a thread read by scrolling the page, oldest first, at reading width. Each message is open, its head over its whole text, or folded to its sender, first line and when it came (`Heard`: the time today, then the day and the time); the account's own messages say "me". The newest and whatever was unread start open; pressing a folded message opens it in place, and pressing an open one's head folds it again, so several can be open at once. A message's text is read as it first opens and kept. Past five messages the middle folds into one "N more" row, keeping the first and the last two in sight, which opens all of it. Opening a conversation with unread mail marks it read. For review opens one message, as before.

The bar: back to the list by its name (Esc); from Inbox, Archive (E), which goes on to the next conversation, and Mark unread (Shift+U), which goes back to the list; where it is in the list, "3 of 19"; previous and next (K and J). The subject is the page's heading, and under it Sift's row is about the newest message: the answer, which starts on the reader's own where they gave one, and its agree check, so answering moves on in one press as For review's rows do; Why, which shows Sift's reason and, in Inbox, the history; and Rule…. Inbox has nothing beside the conversation: the sender's details are their name, a link to their page. Every other list puts What Sift saw, the sender, what else of theirs waits and the history in a column beside it, and opens Why from the start, since answering is the job there.

Under the conversation, Reply (R), Reply all (A) and Forward (F) answer the newest message in the inbox. Each open message has its own, for answering it instead: Reply, and Reply all and Forward in a menu beside it, at the end of its head. Either way the reply box opens at the foot of the conversation, in place of the presses under it, and says which message it answers when that is not the newest ("Replying to Ada Lovelace, Nov 18"). The conversation is the messages Sift recorded: with Inbox on, all of it within the window, what the person sent from Gmail or a phone among it, once the copy has reached it.

## The composer

One composer, in two places (`web/src/views/composer.tsx`): new mail in a narrow panel of its own (`Composer`), opened by C or the rail's Compose on a desktop and Inbox's Compose on a phone; a reply or a forward in the reader, at the foot of the conversation, which stays above it (`InlineComposer`, in a `ReplyBox`), opened by Reply, Reply all and Forward under it or on any open message. Each opens through `useCompose()` (`web/src/lib/compose.ts`): `compose()` for new mail, `compose({ kind: 'reply', messageId, all })` or `compose({ kind: 'forward', messageId })`. Where it is open is in the address: `?compose=new` adds a history entry, so Back closes it; `reply`, `all` or `forward` rewrites the reader's own, so Back goes to the list and the draft waits for the next Reply; it answers the reader's message (`?message=<id>`), or with `answering=<id>` another of its conversation, so a reload keeps the box on the message it answers. Sent, the reply box closes with the focus back on the reader and the reply in the conversation. With Inbox off nothing offers it, and the address opens nothing.

- **From**, when there is more than one address to send as: every mailbox Google lets Sift send from and its aliases (`aliases` in `/api/manage`), and the one it starts on even if it may not yet. New mail starts on the mailbox the switch shows (the primary for All); a reply or a forward on the mailbox its message came to, by the address it was sent to, and offers only that mailbox's addresses.
- **To and Cc**: addresses typed with commas between, `ada@example.org` or `Ada <ada@example.org>`, each checked once the reader has moved past it, with what is wrong said under the field. Cc is behind a press, open from the start on a reply to all. A reply starts To on whoever replies go to (Reply-To, else the sender; mail the person sent, to whoever it went to), and to all with everyone else on it in Cc, the person's own addresses left out.
- **Subject** for new mail and a forward, which starts on its message's with "Fwd: "; a reply's is its message's with "Re: ", shown under the title and set by the server.
- **The text**, plain, at reading size. A forward's starts with its message quoted as Gmail quotes one (From, Date, Subject, To, Cc, then its text), with the reader's place above it. It goes as new mail, a conversation of its own, and without the message's attachments, which Sift does not keep.

Send is the one `ink` press, with ⌘↵ or Ctrl ↵ from anywhere in it; Discard drops the draft, with Undo on its toast. Sent, it closes and a toast says `Sent` under the subject; a refusal or failure keeps it open with the words under the presses. A mailbox Google has not let Sift send from says so, with the press that asks (`/auth/login?grant=send&mailbox=…&to=<this page>`), and Send waits. What is written is kept in this browser (`localStorage`, under who is signed in: one draft for new mail, one per message answered and one per message forwarded) until it is sent or discarded, or they sign out, with the Message-ID of a press of Send that may not have gone.

## Turning it on

Settings has Inbox, off by default (`Mail`, `web/src/views/settings.tsx`), and so does the Inbox screen while it is off, as a `Callout` (`TurnInboxOn`, `web/src/components/inbox-switch.tsx`); Inbox is among the sections only while it is on. Each asks first how far back to keep the mail (`MailWindowDialog`, `web/src/components/inbox-switch.tsx`): the last year, the last three years, or all of it, each with about how many messages, what they take to keep and how long copying them takes (`GET /api/mail/reach`). While on, Settings shows the copy going (how far, about how long is left) or the window kept, and Change… opens the same choice: wider copies what it adds, narrower asks first (a `DeleteButton`) and deletes the text outside it. Turning it off deletes what Sift kept for it, and nothing offers to send. Turning it on does not ask Google for `gmail.send`: the composer asks, for the mailbox it would send from, when it may not yet ([TODO.md](../TODO.md)).

## Data

Inbox is off until the person turns it on (`preferences.mail`, `POST /api/mail/on`, `mail` in `/api/manage`).

| | Where | While |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is in the inbox now, and what is unread | `messages.label_ids`, kept from Gmail's history by every sync (`followLabels`, `src/store/messages.ts`); `messages_inbox` lists it newest first, `messages_thread` finds a thread | Always, for everyone. Stale for what changed while the history cursor had expired |
| Whether Sift may send from a mailbox | `mailboxes.can_send`, from the scopes of the sign-in it holds, set at every sign-in; `canSend` per mailbox in `/api/manage`. `/auth/login?grant=send&mailbox=<address>` (and `&to=<path>` to come back to) asks Google for it | Until a sign-in without it replaces the token |
| The person's mail | A `messages` row each, and its whole text and HTML in `mail_bodies`, sealed like `read_bodies` (`src/store/content.ts`): mail received within the window (`preferences.mail_window`: `year`, `three_years`, `all`), and whatever is in the inbox however old | Inbox is on. Turning it off deletes every row of `mail_bodies` and `mail_copies` in the same write; the records then go after a year, as any record does |
| Mail the person sent | A `messages` row with Gmail's id, conversation and labels for it, decided already so it is never triaged, and what they wrote in `mail_bodies`: from Sift by whichever try of the send finds it went (`recordSent`, `src/store/sent.ts`), from Gmail or a phone by the sync that reads it gaining `SENT` | While Inbox is on |

Three things fill it, each recording a message and its text in one transaction under a share lock on the person's preferences, so turning Inbox off waits for a write under way and its delete finds it, and nothing lands after it (`recordKept`, `keepText`, `src/store/kept.ts`):

- **The copy** (`copyMail`, `src/work/copy.ts`; `mail_copies`, one row a mailbox). Turning Inbox on starts one for each mailbox, and connecting a mailbox while it is on starts its own. It lists `{in:inbox after:<since>} before:<before> -in:drafts -in:chats` (Gmail takes epoch seconds), newest first, 100 ids a page, and reads each message it has no record of whole once (`getMessage`, `format=full`, 60 units: headers, labels and text in one read, where metadata then text would be 80), recording it decided already, so it is never in the triage queue; going through the inbox still decides what of it is in the inbox, as a press. A message recorded without its text has its text read (60); one in the inbox and recorded is left to the pass, which keeps inbox text newest first (`keepMailBodies`), so the two never read the same message. Spam, Trash, drafts and chats are not mail (`NOT_MAIL`, `src/gmail/sync.ts`). The page's token is kept once its messages are, so a step cut off at its limit lists the same page again and passes over what it recorded: safe to repeat, and a crash loses nothing. It is a run of its own a mailbox (`copy:<mailbox's number>`), begun by each tick while Inbox is on and the sign-in has not expired; several mailboxes copy side by side, each under its own quota. `before` is when Inbox was turned on: sync records what came after.
- **Sync, wider.** While Inbox is on, history's new mail outside the inbox (a filter's, mail archived as it arrived) and mail gaining `SENT` (what the person sent from Gmail or a phone) is read whole and recorded the same way, untriaged, and begins a pass as new mail does (`elsewhere`, `pullChanges`). New inbox mail is read whole too, once (60 units, not 20 for its headers and 60 more for its text), recorded and triaged as before, and its text kept as it is recorded, so the model reads it from there.
- **The pass**, for inbox mail it has a record of and no text: 50 a pass after triage, taken from what Sift read with Inbox off in the last 30 days (`read_bodies`) before Gmail, and anything a pass reads from Gmail is kept here as it is read (`keepMailBodies`, `readingOnce`, `src/work/bodies.ts`); the look begins a pass while some is missing (`mailBodiesDue`).

The window moves with time: "the last year" keeps a year back from today, and retention lets go of what falls out of it ([architecture.md](architecture.md), Retention). Widening it starts a copy from the new start to the old (or moves the copy under way back to it); narrowing deletes the text outside it in the same write, stops a copy under way at the new start, and leaves the records to the next upkeep. The text is all of it up to 200,000 characters (`MAX_MAIL_CHARS`, `src/gmail/body.ts`), not the 4,000 the model is shown, and the model and the other tiers read it from here instead of Gmail (`heldText`), and the search from its index (Search, above). What it costs: [runtime.md](runtime.md#what-it-costs).

The list is `GET /api/mail/threads` (`listThreads`, `src/dashboard/queries/mail.ts`): conversations with a message in the inbox now, by `messages_inbox`, or for a search or Ask's senders or first day every conversation Sift keeps, all their recorded messages by `messages_thread`, 50 a page from `before` (the last one's newest time and thread). `view`, `label` and `unread` narrow it in SQL. A search (`q`) reads through at most 200 conversations the index lets through after `before`, and answers what matched, with `next` to read on from. A conversation is `GET /api/mail/thread?thread=<id>` (`conversation`), found in whichever of the person's mailboxes holds it as `/api/message` finds a message, and a message's whole text is `/api/message`'s, which reads `mail_bodies` before the copies For review keeps. Neither reads Gmail.

The reader's own presses are `POST /api/mail/change` (`{ accountId, messageIds, change: 'archive' | 'read' | 'unread' }`), through `Controls.changeMail` to the executor's `press` (`src/actions/executor.ts`): only a message in the inbox now, its labels read again first, the change written to `actions` before the call (`archive` with no decision, `read`, `unread`) and checked against Gmail's answer, whose labels go straight into `messages.label_ids` (`recordLabels`). They act whether Sift is on or not, as an answer does: they are the person's own, not Sift's. So they carry no `sift/did/` label, a correction never takes them back (`lastStandingChange` is Sift's alone), and they are no answer to learn from. Undo on the toast is the executor's undo of each change, which puts the labels in the record back too. Gmail is asked twice a message: a quota unit of 5 for the read, 5 for the change.

The page sends through `POST /api/mail/send` (`SendRequest`, `src/dashboard/types.ts`), which takes the mailbox, the Message-ID the page made for the press, From, To, Cc and the text, and either a subject (new mail and a forward) or the message it answers. For a reply the route reads that message's record (`replyTo`, `src/dashboard/queries/reply.ts`) for its conversation, its subject with "Re: ", and the Message-IDs it follows (its own, after its References, at most 20 kept: the first and the newest), and the page's word on any of them is not asked for. It hands that to `Controls.send`: `sendMail` (`src/actions/send.ts`) builds plain text in UTF-8 from `{ from, to, cc, subject, text, inReplyTo, references }`, refusing a header value that could break a line and any address that is not plain, and sends it through `Mailbox.send`. It refuses unless Inbox is on, the mailbox may send and `from` is one of its addresses. The page makes a press's Message-ID when Send is first pressed, keeps it with the draft until the draft changes, and gives it again on a retry, which first looks for the earlier try in Gmail, as a digest does. Gmail's search can lag a send by seconds, so while an earlier try is still waiting on Gmail, or failed less than two minutes ago (`SETTLE_MS`) and is not found, the retry is refused rather than sent. Once Gmail has taken it, the press succeeds even if writing it to the record fails: sync records it later, and an error there would invite a second send. Each try is an `actions` row of type `send`, written before the call; a send cannot be undone.
